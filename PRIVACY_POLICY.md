# Privacy Policy — CSI South Kerala Songbook

**Effective date:** 3 October 2026

CSI South Kerala Songbook is designed as an offline songbook application.

## Information collected
The app is designed to operate without requiring an account. Songbook content, favorites, recent-song information, theme preferences, and reading preferences are stored locally on the device.

The app does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, or payment information.

## Sharing
The app includes a user-initiated sharing feature for song text. When you choose to share content, Android may provide the selected content to another application. This action is initiated by you.

## Internet access
The core songbook is intended to work offline. No online account is required for reading the bundled songbook content.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes
This policy may be updated if app functionality changes. The effective date above will be updated when a material change is made.
